NYC Apartment Check

One address in, everything the city publishes about the building and block out.

    What this checks

    • HPD: housing violations, tenant complaints, housing court cases, evictions, owner & managing agent
    • DOB: building violations, ECB fines, complaints, permits and DOB NOW job filings
    • 311: service requests at the building and around the block
    • NYPD: reported crimes within a radius you choose, compared to the city average
    • Rent stabilization: a likelihood estimate plus where to confirm it

    Data comes live from NYC Open Data and NYC Planning GeoSearch. Most datasets refresh daily; some lag by days or weeks.

    Not affiliated with the City of New York. Data is provided as-is from public city datasets and may be incomplete or delayed — always confirm on the official sites before signing a lease.
